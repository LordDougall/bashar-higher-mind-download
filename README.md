## Core Model

**Higher Mind** — Conceiver  
**Physical Brain** — Receiver  
**Physical Mind** — Perceiver

The Higher Mind originates ideas, inspirations, and conceptions and directs higher-frequency vibrational information into the brain.  
The physical brain receives and translates this into neurological, neuroelectric, and neurochemical form.  
The physical mind perceives and experiences the result.

## Mechanism

- The pineal gland functions as the interface / nodal point for translation.
- DMT (dimethyltryptamine) is the primary neurotransmitter involved, vibrationally keyed to receive higher-frequency information.
- The pineal regulates the quantity and vibration of DMT (described as the tuning dial / gatekeeper / regulator).
- Serotonin and melatonin also participate in the translation process.
- These create neurochemical and neuroelectric templates that the physical mind accesses.
- Belief systems in the physical mind then filter what is perceived.

Higher Mind Download Process

Mapped strictly from the session: “Your Brain Is a Receiver?! The Higher Mind Download”

Conceiver

Higher Mind

▼

Originates all ideas, inspirations, creations, and conceptions. These do not come from the physical mind.

“Anything that is what you call an inspiration, an idea, a creation, a conception — it does not actually originate in the physical mind. It originates in the higher mind.”

The higher mind directs higher-frequency vibrational information into the brain.

↓
Receiver

Physical Brain

▼

Receives the download and sets it up neurologically, neuroelectrically, and neurochemically so the physical mind can perceive and experience it.

“That is then downloaded through the physical brain and is then in a sense set up in the physical brain in a neurological, neuroelectric way, neurochemical way to create the physical mind that then has the perception, the experience, the feedback, the application of what you call physical reality.”
↓
Perceiver

Physical Mind (Personality Construct)

▼

Perceives the reality that results from the application of the downloaded information. It experiences inspiration, imagination, thought patterns, etc. It does not conceive the original idea.

“Physical mind is the perceiver of the reality that results from the application of the information downloaded from the higher mind.”
Interface / Nodal Point

Pineal Gland

▼

The neurological and neurochemical interface that allows translation of higher vibrational information so the physical mind can access it. Described as the seat of the soul.

“In your physical brain, there is a nodal point, a nodule that you refer to as the pineal gland… It is the interface. It is the neurological, neurochemical interface that allows for the translation of higher vibrational information into the brain…”
Primary Receiver

DMT (Dimethyltryptamine)

▼

The primary neurotransmitter produced by the pineal. Vibrationally keyed to receive higher-frequency vibrations. The pineal regulates its quantity and vibration.

Tuning Dial / Gatekeeper / Regulator
Determines how much focus is in physical versus non-physical reality and the appropriate amount for receiving the higher information.
“The pineal gland regulates this molecule and its vibration to determine exactly how and to what degree you are or are not focused in physical or non-physical reality. It is the gatekeeper. It is the regulator. It is the tuning dial…”
Translation Support

Serotonin & Melatonin

▼

Where and when necessary, the vibrational energy received by DMT is translated into these other neurotransmitters. Together with DMT they are released into cerebrospinal fluid, bloodstream, brain cells, and body cells.

“Then also, where and when necessary, translates that vibrational energy into other neurochemical transmitters such as serotonin and melatonin and along with dimethyltryptamine in a sense dumps these neurotransmitters into your cerebrospinal fluid, into your bloodstream, into the cells of your brain, into the cells of your body in such a manner as to then set up the template neurochemically and then neuroelectrically…”
Result

Templates → Physical Mind Access

▼

Once the information is set up in neurochemical and neuroelectric form, the physical mind has access to it and experiences it as inspiration, imagination, thought patterns, and so on.

After Translation

Belief Systems & Definitions

▼

After the brain has translated the higher information into neurochemical/neuroelectric form, the physical mind applies it according to previously crystallized or solidified patterns. These patterns are called belief systems and definitions.

“These crystallizations, these solidifications of patterns of information that the physical mind has received before that it has decided are appropriate or pertinent or relevant to it are called belief systems and definitions.”

These crystallized beliefs regulate how much of the higher-frequency information can actually be accessed and perceived. Constrictive or limiting ones reduce the efficacy of the download.

Information is always passed through these belief systems after it has been translated. Clarifying them improves the ability to receive the original higher information more clearly.
Permission

Permission Slips

▼

Tools, techniques, and rituals are simply permission slips you give yourself based on your belief system. Whatever you are attracted to is keyed to your vibrational makeup and will work best for you.

Physical Mind Role

Why It Cannot Control the “How”

▼

The physical mind is the perceiver, not the conceiver. It is not designed to know or control how things will unfold. Attempting to force control exhausts it. Its role is to perceive and experience.

Signal

Follow Highest Excitement

▼

Excitement, joy, passion, love, and creativity are indications of guidance from the higher mind. Acting on the highest of these completes the process. Inspiration alone is incomplete without application.

Receiver Quality

Becoming a Clearer Receiver

▼

Trust the higher mind, clarify belief systems, support the brain’s ability to translate (including pineal focus, especially before sleep), and act on the guidance that comes through. Information is always being sent; clearer reception reduces self-interference.
